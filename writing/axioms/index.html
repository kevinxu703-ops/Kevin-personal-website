Where the series goes next

Later parts will consider the limits of formal systems and the philosophical questions that remain when mathematics turns to models, structures, and the world beyond its axioms. Titles and order may change as the essays develop.

Technical extensions from individual essays will be gathered in a single appendix at the end of the series. It will appear here when ready.
